# The Adventures of Laila & Salma 🌸

A cozy, Studio Ghibli-inspired 3D web adventure series starring **Laila (9)**, **Salma (5)**, and **Mochi the Shiba Inu**, built with a modular shared Three.js r128 engine and playable on laptops, tablets, and phones.

## Playable Worlds

### 🗼 World 1: Tokyo — Sakura-cho Neighborhood
- Walk sunny Sakura-cho with **Ichiban Ramen** (sit-and-eat animation + boosts), **Harajuku / Tokyo Boutique**, **Sakura Elementary School** (Laila's Grade-3 Quiz + Salma's Picture Quiz), **Gachapon Capsule Toy Machine**, **Japanese Drink & Snack Vending Machine**, **Pocket Park** (Slide, Swing, Hopscotch), and **Laila & Salma's Room** customization.
- Find **5 hidden neighborhood cats**, play **30s Fetch & Tag with Mochi**, and admire **Tokyo Tower** and distant **Mt. Fuji** on the skyline.
- Board the **Tokyo Shinkansen Station** bullet train to travel to Kyoto!

### ⛩️ World 2: Kyoto — Sakurako Shrine & Arashiyama
- Cross the **East–West Arched Cedar Bridge** over the **Excavated Koi Lake** (with swimming koi, gliding ducks, bobbing wooden rowboat, and an interactive **Koi & Duck Feeding Dock**).
- Visit **Sakura Sushi (`桜寿司`)** lakeside *yatai* counter, walk through the **Vermilion Senbon Torii Tunnel** into **Sakurako Shrine (`桜湖神社`)** (with *Chozuya*, interactive **Omikuji Lucky Fortunes & Ema Wishes**, elevated courtyard, *Komainu* lions, Main Hall, sacred *Goshinboku* cedar & *Jizo*).
- Stroll through the **Arashiyama Bamboo Grove**, sip sweets at **Uji Matcha & Wagashi Tea House (`宇治茶屋`)**, try on silk kimonos & festival fox masks at the **Kyoto Kimono Boutique (`京都着物`)**, take quizzes at **Kyoto Temple Academy (`寺子屋`)**, decorate **Laila & Salma's Kyoto Ryokan Suite**, and befriend **5 hidden Kitsune foxes**!

## Shared Multi-World Features
- **Travel Passport (`🗺️` / `P`)**: Jump between Tokyo & Kyoto anytime, track 8 collectible Passport Stamps, and preview upcoming worlds (**Ancient Egypt** & **Viking Scandinavia**).
- **Shared Progression**: Coins, XP, Level, Outfits, and Passport Stamps carry seamlessly across worlds.
- **Mobile-Safe Technical Budget**: Three.js r128, strict 4-light WebGL budget, emissive lanterns/windows, `devicePixelRatio <= 1.5`, shared procedural `256x256` textures, and `try/catch`-isolated zone builders.

## Controls
- **Move:** `WASD` / Arrow Keys (or Mobile Touch Joystick)
- **Run:** `Shift` / `R` (or `RUN` button)
- **Jump:** `Space` (or `Jump` button)
- **Interact:** `E` (or tap the prompt / `E • Act` button)
- **World Map & Travel Passport:** `P` (or `🗺️`)
- **Camera:** Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, `C` (or `📷`) for Laila / Salma Front View
- **Sister High-Five & Animal Hint:** `V` (or `👋`)
- **Mini-Map Toggle:** `M` (or tap mini-map)
